# Project requirements

School: Border Guard Public School and College, Sylhet

## Technology

- Backend: Go
- Frontend: HTML, CSS, JavaScript
- Database: to be decided at the data-persistence step

## Version 1 modules

1. Admin dashboard: student, teacher, staff, class, section, attendance, unpaid-fee, and recent-activity summaries.
2. Student management: admission, profiles, IDs, class/section/roll, guardian details, promotion, and transfer history.
3. Teacher management: profiles, employee IDs, subject/class assignments, attendance, salary records, and timetables.
4. Attendance: daily present/absent/late records, monthly reports, and low-attendance alerts.
5. Exams and results: schedules, subject marks, GPA/grade calculation, marksheets, report cards, and publishing.
6. Fees: admission and tuition fees, payment history, receipts, due tracking, fines, and discounts.

The team will build and commit one section at a time.
